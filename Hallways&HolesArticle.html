Holey Hallways

Written: januaRy 5th 2022

Please travel with caution as certain hallways often contain unstable infrastructure with holes that individuals can easily fall into. It should be noted that these specifics are only made up a smaller section of the larger hallway area and are unusual to encounter. However if one does come across this area, please make sure to carefully look down to avoid falling, just don’t directly look into the holes themselves as doing so can instill a sense of deep seeded anxiety. Almost all instances of recorded holed hallways seem to be at least 20 ft in the air.
The hallways as a whole sprawling mazes of mono colored hallways are a part of the larger hallways system, which acts as the in-between spaces of the actual "zone" of themed areas. While the Hallways are generally safer as they have a much simpler architectural layout compared to themed areas. The Hallways are also typically devoid of [REDACTED] meaning little concern of dealing with dangerous encounters. Overall likely any zone poses some level of risk so please be cautious and alert when navigated through the Convergence zone.
